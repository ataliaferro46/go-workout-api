Body composition

If you're muscular, BMI overstates your body fat and Mifflin-St Jeor underestimates your BMR. Entering body-fat % switches the calorie engine to Katch-McArdle, which is meaningfully more accurate for athletes.

Pick the silhouette closest to what you see in the mirror. Close enough is plenty accurate — Katch-McArdle tolerates ±2-3% error well.

Workout time

Tell us when you typically train and the nutrition page will flag the meal closest before your workout (carb-heavy, low-fat) and the meal after (protein + carbs, low-fat).

Drives your daily calorie target. Cuts get more protein per lb to preserve muscle in a deficit; bulks get less to leave calories for carbs/fat.
